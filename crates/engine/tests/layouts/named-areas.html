Beans at last

After three weeks of cold nights the runner beans have finally found the canes, and the first flowers opened on Tuesday. The broad beans, sown in November, are already past their best.

The slugs have taken most of the lettuce again. The copper tape slows them down but does not stop them, and the beer traps catch more snails than slugs.

Next week: the leeks go out, and the shed roof needs a new sheet of felt before the autumn.
