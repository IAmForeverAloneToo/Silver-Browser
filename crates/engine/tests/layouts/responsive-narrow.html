Printing the web

A page laid out for the screen is cut into pages for paper, and each page shows a slice of it. Where it is cut follows rules the author can give, keeping a heading with its paragraph or a figure whole.

The same page answers the window it is shown in: narrow, its parts stack; wider, they sit side by side; wide, a sidebar joins the article.

Breakpoints

Media queries choose rules by the window's width.

Layers

Cascade layers order rules before their selectors do.

Support

Feature queries keep what a browser cannot do aside.
