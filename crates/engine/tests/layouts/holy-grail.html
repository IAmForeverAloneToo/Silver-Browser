Three columns, one flexible

The navigation and the aside keep their widths, and the article between them takes the rest of the row, however wide the window is. The columns are as tall as the tallest of them, and the footer stays at the bottom of the page when there is little to read.

An article's minimum width is zero here, so a long word cannot push the aside off the page.
